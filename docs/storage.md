# Storage

## Overview

Storage is a fundamental component of the Nyxthorh Lab.

Every virtual machine, application, dataset, and backup ultimately depends on reliable storage. The storage platform is designed to provide stability, consistency, and recoverability while supporting continuous experimentation.

Reliable storage enables the infrastructure to evolve without compromising data integrity.

---

## Objectives

The storage platform is designed to achieve several long-term objectives:

- Reliable data storage
- Predictable performance
- Data integrity
- Simplified management
- Scalability
- Efficient recovery

The focus is not on maximizing capacity, but on maintaining a dependable platform for all workloads.

---

## Data Organization

Different types of data have different requirements.

Virtual machines, application data, surveillance recordings, documentation, and backups are managed independently whenever practical.

Separating data according to its purpose simplifies administration, improves maintainability, and reduces operational risk.

---

## Data Integrity

Protecting data is more important than maximizing storage efficiency.

Storage decisions prioritize consistency, recoverability, and long-term reliability over short-term optimization.

Maintaining confidence in stored data is considered essential to the overall infrastructure.

---

## Growth

The storage platform is expected to grow together with the lab.

Additional services, larger datasets, and new workloads will increase storage requirements over time.

The architecture is designed to support gradual expansion without requiring major redesign.

---

## Operational Philosophy

Storage should remain predictable.

Capacity planning, maintenance, and monitoring are treated as ongoing operational tasks rather than reactive activities.

A storage platform should remain stable even as the surrounding infrastructure changes.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Storage technologies continue to evolve.

As the lab grows, storage architecture will be reviewed and improved to meet new requirements while maintaining simplicity, reliability, and operational efficiency.
